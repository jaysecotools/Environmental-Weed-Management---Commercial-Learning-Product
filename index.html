Why this programme matters

Controlling weeds is not the same as managing them in context.

Controlling weeds

Treating the target plant in front of you — often with limited reference to the surrounding site, its values or what happens next.

Managing weeds in context

Understanding the site, selecting an appropriate intervention, minimising harm to environmental values, preventing further spread and supporting long-term ecosystem recovery.

What learners will gain

Practical capability you can apply on the next job.

Programme overview

Seven concise modules

Approximately 3–4 hours of online learning, subject to pilot validation. Module names and descriptions are provisional until the curriculum is formally approved.

Who should participate

Built for contractors and the people who engage them.

Learning approach

Self-directed, scenario-led, practically grounded.

Completion

A certificate of completion, honestly framed
